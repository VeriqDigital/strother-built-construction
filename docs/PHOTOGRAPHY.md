These are published gallery photographs, not the site's generic service-header images. Labels describe only visible rooms and finishes; no location, cost, date, or project scope is inferred. Files are stored locally, not hotlinked. The shower photo is cropped by 112 pixels at its top edge to exclude the photographer's hand; the work itself is unchanged. All other new files are unaltered originals with responsive CSS crops. No generative editing, retouching, or stock imagery was used.

## Selection decisions

- Preserve the supplied white-kitchen hero and closing CTA as the existing visual bookends.
- Replace the repeated brick-look bathroom imagery with connected finished rooms, a glass shower, and the blue-cabinet kitchen.
- Replace the empty basement gallery photo with a furnished living space. Use the label “Living space” without claiming an unverified remodeling scope.
- Retain the supplied completed gray-tile bathtub photo in the service section as a different bathroom example.
- The blue kitchen appears once as the main gallery photograph and once in a tighter cabinetry/countertop crop in the trust section. This limited reuse is preferable to unfinished or cluttered alternatives from the available collection.
- Reject exposed framing, active work, materials and tools, incomplete cabinetry, the cluttered blue bar, and photos with missing finish details. Do not use the site's stock-style service headers, awards graphic, competitor images, or stock libraries.
- Originals are web-resolution (approximately 768–1024 pixels across). Higher-resolution client originals would improve large-screen sharpness; no artificial upscaling was used.
