### Project summary

Strother Built Construction wants to simplify an existing site that currently promotes a broad mix of contracting and repair services. The new direction should position the company for larger, higher-end residential remodeling projects by focusing the homepage on five priority services, using stronger project presentation, and replacing keyword-heavy copy with clearer trust-, quality-, and craftsmanship-oriented messaging.

Josh contacted Veriq through a free homepage mockup offer. No production rebuild, migration, or full-site scope is approved yet.

## 2. Project Goals

### Primary goal

Show how Strother can be repositioned as a credible, quality-focused remodeling contractor for substantial residential projects in the Des Moines metro.

### Secondary goals

- Make the five target project types immediately clear.
- Let project photography and finished work carry more of the sales message.
- Increase perceived trust and professionalism without inventing proof.
- Create a cleaner, calmer, more premium visual system while retaining recognizable brand cues.
- Give qualified homeowners an obvious path to discuss a project.
- Establish an SEO structure that serves people first and avoids the current keyword-heavy feel.

### Success should feel like

A homeowner considering a kitchen, bathroom, basement, whole-home remodel, or addition should quickly understand that Strother handles meaningful remodeling projects, see work that creates confidence, understand why the company is worth contacting, and know how to start a conversation.

## 3. Audience

### Primary customer

Homeowners in the Greater Des Moines area who are planning a substantial renovation or addition and who care about workmanship, trust, communication, and confidence in the finished result—not simply the lowest bid.

### Likely customer mindset

- They may be investing significantly in a home they plan to keep.
- They want proof that the contractor can execute the desired level of work.
- They are comparing several local remodelers.
- They may be anxious about quality, disruption, communication, budget clarity, and schedule management.
- They want to see relevant project examples before initiating contact.

### Questions the homepage must answer

- Does Strother handle the kind of project I am planning?
- Does the work look like the quality level I want?
- Does the company serve my area?
- Why should I trust Strother with a major remodel?
- What is the process of starting a project?
- What happens after I contact the company?

The last two questions require client confirmation before detailed public claims are written.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

The current homepage concept is complete when:

- The five primary services are unmistakable.
- Handyman services and small-repair positioning are absent.
- The visual system feels cleaner and more premium than the current site.
- The design remains recognizably Strother through restrained brand cues.
- Project imagery is the primary visual proof.
- Copy emphasizes trust, quality, and craftsmanship without keyword stuffing.
- No unsupported facts or borrowed competitor claims appear.
- The main CTA is obvious and the projects path is easy to find.
- Mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts are intentional.
- Applicable lint, type-check, and build commands pass.
- No live production systems were changed.
