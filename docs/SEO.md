## 1. SEO Objective

### Current mockup implementation

The user's current brief authorizes metadata and factual structured data for `/`, `/services`, `/about`, and `/contact`. Keep this concept noindex in metadata, HTTP headers, and robots.txt. Do not emit production canonicals or a production sitemap before launch approval. A trusted `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` may set the preview origin for social images; the local fallback is `http://localhost:3000`. The schema includes only business name, existing business URL, confirmed email, five confirmed offerings, and the confirmed broad Des Moines market. Omit unconfirmed phone, address, hours, credentials, reviews, and individual service-area cities from schema.

### Primary objective

Earn qualified organic visibility for substantial residential remodeling projects in the Des Moines metro while presenting the company as trustworthy, quality-focused, and relevant to homeowners—not as a broad handyman service.

### Primary organic conversion

Project inquiry through a future approved contact experience.

### Geographic focus

**Primary market:** Des Moines metro

**Exact service area:** Existing-site list recorded below; reconfirm before production

### Strategic guardrail

The client explicitly wants less keyword-heavy wording. SEO must come from clear page topics, useful content, project proof, internal linking, technical quality, and accurate local information—not repeated city/service phrases.

## 8. Content Strategy

### Project content is the highest-value SEO asset

Future case studies should include, when approved:

- Project type and general location
- Homeowner challenge
- Scope of work
- Planning or construction constraints
- Materials/finish details
- Before and after images
- What changed in function or use
- Internal links to the relevant service

### Educational content

Only publish articles that answer real homeowner questions and demonstrate actual expertise, such as:

- What to plan before a major kitchen remodel
- Basement finishing considerations in Iowa homes
- When an addition makes sense compared with moving
- How whole-home remodeling is sequenced

Avoid generic AI-generated posts written only to target keywords.
